The CANN extension builder retains its CANN Open Software License Agreement
Version 2.0 notice. Its source is the pinned official image's
cann_ops_transformer package. The modification supports importing prebuilt
extensions under runveil; original operator implementations are unchanged.

Adaptations cover target hidden-state capture, DFlash2 V1 integration and
hybrid-cache handling, pure-text weight mapping, bounded indexer RoPE and
container-local CANN extension initialization. No model weights are included.
